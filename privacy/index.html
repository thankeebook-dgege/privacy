ThankeeBook Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026.

This Privacy Policy covers the ThankeeBook mobile app (com.thankeebook.app) and the website thankeebook.com.

Developer and data controller: Danielov Albert Vardani, individual entrepreneur, Republic of Armenia, tax number 20421445. Privacy contact: thankeebook@gmail.com

The app has no ads and no analytics. We do not sell your data.

Data we collect and why

DataWhy
Account: your email address; your name and profile picture link if you sign in with Google; a password hash if you sign in with emailto sign you in
What you save: folders, links, titles, notes, tags, reminders, the text of saved article copies, and your app settingsto keep your library and sync it between your devices
Subscription status and expiry dateto unlock paid features
Counts of your AI requeststo enforce usage limits and prevent abuse

The microphone is used only when you dictate a note or search by voice. The audio is processed by your phone's own speech service (on most Android devices, Google); we receive only the resulting text.

A profile photo you choose stays on your phone and is not sent to us. The app does not access your contacts, location, calls, SMS or photo library.

Who receives your data

  • Supabase Inc. hosts our database and sign-in service on servers in Frankfurt, Germany, and processes data only on our behalf.
  • Google provides Google sign-in and handles subscription payments through Google Play. We never receive your card details.
  • AI providers: Google (Gemini) and Mistral AI. Only when you use an AI feature, the link, title and your note for that entry are sent to produce the answer. We do not store the content of these requests.
  • Websites you save links from (for example YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo). To show a preview, check a link or save an article copy, the app contacts that website, which sees the link and your internet address.

We do not share your data with anyone else.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). On the server, each account can access only its own data, and passwords are stored only as hashes. The PIN for locked folders is kept on your phone as a hash and is never sent to us; it locks content inside the app but does not encrypt it.

How long data is kept

  • What you save is kept until you delete it or your account.
  • A deleted entry is erased from the server at once and stays in Trash on your phone for 30 days.
  • AI usage counters are kept for up to 13 months.
  • If an account is blocked for abuse, its email address stays on a block list until the block is lifted.

How to delete your data

In the app: Profile → Personal details → "Delete account".

By email: write to thankeebook@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. We complete the request within 30 days.

The account is closed at once and signed out on all devices. The account and everything saved in it are erased from the server 14 days later; until then you can cancel the deletion by signing in again. Deleting the account also erases the data on the phone where you requested it. A copy on another phone remains until you uninstall the app there.

Your rights

You can export your data (Profile → "Export everything to a file"), correct or delete it in the app, switch off syncing, and ask us what we hold about you. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the data protection authority of your country.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Website

thankeebook.com uses no cookies, analytics or forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare, which see your internet address and the time of the request in their technical logs.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
