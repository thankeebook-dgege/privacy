How to delete your ThankeeBook account and data

App: ThankeeBook (com.thankeebook.app). Developer: Danielov Albert Vardani, individual entrepreneur · Republic of Armenia · TIN 20421445.

In the app: Profile → Personal details → "Delete account".

By email: write to thankeebook@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. We complete the request within 30 days.

The account is closed at once and signed out on all devices. The account and everything saved in it are erased from the server 14 days later; until then you can cancel the deletion by signing in again. Deleting the account also erases the data on the phone where you requested it. A copy on another phone remains until you uninstall the app there.
